# Ticket: Unit Management Panel and Root Organization Configuration

## Implementation Owner

GitHub Copilot (implementation in this session).

## State and Approved Architecture

This ticket is in **REVIEW** after implementation and verification of the sole
remaining safe Unit deletion workflow. The independent profile,
authorized-user/grant, child-create/move, and RootUnit catalog portions are
implemented and verified. The human-approved limited hard-delete decision
below authorizes deletion of otherwise-unused leaf Units only; it does not
authorize archival or deletion of protected history.

Approved decisions:

- Units owns Unit profile, authorized-user and grant administration, structural
  create/move/delete workflows, and their management UI. Structural operations
  are operation-specific Units server workflows; no generic client-consumable
  `canManageStructure` boolean is the sole authorization mechanism.
- RootUnit owns Rank and Medal catalogs. The narrow Units-owned
  `canManageRootSettings(userId, rootUnitId)`-equivalent applies only to the
  current RootUnit owner and only to those catalogs.
- Authorized-user memberships are history-preserving periods with `endedAt`,
  one active membership per User/Unit, a new row on re-add, and preserved grant
  lineage. Membership end revokes active grants transactionally; existing
  source revocation invalidates dependent grants. Ordinary removal cannot end
  the current Commander/level-0 membership.
- Unit profile metadata is Units-owned and uses existing `MANAGE_UNIT`
  semantics. Image/flag/icon values are references only; file upload and
  storage services remain out of scope and require future RED approval.
- Unit hard deletion is limited to an otherwise-unused non-RootUnit leaf with
  no children, Player/Event or other protected history, additional or
  historical authorized-user memberships, or grants/delegation history. Its
  sole active Commander/level-0 bootstrap membership is deleted atomically with
  the Unit; no archive/tombstone state is introduced.

All approved feature areas, including limited safe leaf hard deletion, have
been implemented and verified. A Unit may be hard-deleted only if it is not a
RootUnit, has no children, has no Player/Event or other protected history, and
its only authorized-user state is the current active level-0 Commander
bootstrap membership with no grants. That bootstrap row may be removed
atomically with the Unit. Historical memberships and grants are never deleted
to make a Unit eligible. Unit archival/tombstones remain out of scope.

## Objective

Deliver a full vertical slice for managing a persisted Unit through a dedicated
Unit Management Panel, and expose a RootUnit-only organization-settings area to
the RootUnit owner. The panel consolidates Unit profile, authorized-user,
authority, and hierarchy administration while retaining the existing Units
authority model. Root organization settings provide a CRUD catalog of Ranks and
Medals. This is not a second authorization model, a Commander superuser path,
or backend/UI scaffolding without usable workflows.

## Main Success Scenario

1. An authenticated User opens management for a persisted Unit they are
   authorized to manage.
2. The server resolves the User from the existing Auth.js session and enforces
   the existing Units-owned semantic authority separately for each protected
   operation. UI visibility is only presentation.
3. The User updates permitted Unit profile fields, administers authorized Users
   and their permitted grants, and performs permitted hierarchy operations.
4. The public Unit directory and detail page reflect profile changes; current
   hierarchy and historical records remain valid.
5. The RootUnit owner opens organization settings and manages that RootUnit's
   Rank and Medal catalogs.
6. A child-Unit manager without RootUnit-owner authority is denied every
   RootUnit catalog read/write operation, regardless of their normal Unit
   permissions.

## Context and Pre-Implementation Baseline

- **Unit ownership:** BCR-20260926-003 approves Units ownership of Unit profile,
  authorized-user/grant administration, structural workflows, and management
  UI. The module manifest is synchronized by this ticket and the approved
  workflows are implemented here.
- **Schema baseline:** At ticket start, `Unit` persisted `name`, parent/root
  links, required Commander, and timestamps. It had no description,
  flag/image/icon, Discord invite, or external group link. `RootUnit` is keyed
  by its designated Unit and has no separate owner column. Rank and Medal
  models are now implemented under this ticket.
- **Owner semantics:** Under ADR-20260915-002, RootUnit owner means the
  Commander of the designated RootUnit Unit; no separate owner relationship is
  persisted. Commander status, including RootUnit ownership, does not grant any
  operational permission. RootUnit creation/designation, initial owner
  assignment, and ownership transfer are the bounded website-admin operations
  in ADR-20260915-003; that website-admin allowlist is not the requested
  RootUnit settings capability.
- **Existing authorization:** `canManageUnit(userId, unitId)` and
  `canManageAuthorizedUsers(userId, unitId)` are server-only semantic booleans.
  The latter establishes permission coverage only; each user/grant mutation
  must additionally enforce hierarchy, scope, applicable authority-level
  comparisons, delegation limits, and Commander rules. Commander is not an
  operational bypass. BCR-20260926-003 approves operation-specific Units
  structural workflows; a generic `canManageStructure` boolean is not their
  sole authorization boundary.
- **Delegation and revocation:** ADR-20260915-002 defines ordinary scope
  narrowing, structural delegation, authority levels, revocation invalidation,
  and Commander checks. ADR-20260926-005 approves membership periods with
  nullable `endedAt`, one active row per `(userId, unitId)`, and a new row on
  re-add. Ending membership revokes its active grants transactionally, retains
  all grant/delegation rows, and makes grants on an ended membership
  ineffective. Ticket 19 implements this lifecycle in the Prisma schema and
  forward migration.
- **Structural invariants:** ADR-20260915-002 requires atomic child creation
  with an existing initial Commander and level-0 membership, same-RootUnit
  moves, cycle rejection, preservation/recalculation of authority after moves,
  and leaf-only hard deletion with protected-history checks. Subtree deletion
  and Unit archival are excluded. EventParticipation lifecycle protection is
  partly deferred; existing restrictive references must not be bypassed or
  silently cascaded.
- **Read-side/assets baseline:** At ticket start, `/units` and
  `/units/[unitId]` were public read surfaces; the detail page read a Unit's
  name, parent, children, and roster. Ticket 19 adds profile reads and the Unit
  management route. The repository has static Event map artwork under
  `public/maps`, not a reusable Unit image upload or storage mechanism.
- **Ticket baseline:** Ticket 13 established the Units authority capability
  boundary. Ticket 14 implemented Players, membership history, and rosters.
  Tickets 15 and 16 established EventParticipation and Event ownership/
  authorization; Ticket 17 implemented Event management. Ticket 18 is the open
  Events calendar presentation ticket. Do not redesign or write Players or
  Events as a convenience.

## Product Requirements

### Management access and Unit profile

- Provide a dedicated usable management experience for a Unit, reached from
  appropriate existing Unit surfaces when the viewer has relevant authority.
- Allow Unit name, description, flag/image/icon representation, Discord invite,
  and external group link management through Units-owned workflows.
- Enforce `MANAGE_UNIT` for ordinary Unit metadata. It does not grant hierarchy,
  Commander, or authorized-user administration.
- Reflect saved profile data on the applicable public Unit read-side.
- A simple reference/value-based image representation may be chosen within the
  existing architecture. Do not introduce binary upload, a shared storage
  provider, or an external service. If a usable image workflow requires one,
  escalate that subfeature as RED and continue independent work.

### Authorized users, authority, and grants

- Provide a useful list of authorized Users and their local authority level,
  permission grants, and scope information.
- Support adding an existing website User, removing/revoking an authorized
  membership where the approved persistence lifecycle permits, and changing an
  authority level where the actor is allowed.
- Support creating and revoking only grants the actor may delegate. Show
  ordinary scope and the structural-anchor nature of `MANAGE_STRUCTURE` clearly.
- Use `MANAGE_AUTHORIZED_USERS` plus all applicable same-RootUnit, hierarchy,
  scope, strict authority-level, delegation-ceiling, and Commander checks.
  Equal-authority peers may not modify or remove one another. Retain unrelated
  memberships/grants; source revocation must continue to invalidate dependent
  grants without changing the resolver's established behavior.
- Do not implement a new role/RBAC model, infer authority from panel access, or
  trust client-submitted User IDs, permissions, scopes, or authority.
- End membership by setting its end timestamp; retain the historical row and
  all attached grants, revoking active grants transactionally. Re-addition
  creates a distinct active membership period. Do not hard-delete or reuse an
  ended membership or grant lineage.
- The ordinary removal workflow must reject the current Commander/level-0
  membership. Commander replacement remains subject to the separate approved
  Commander rules and is not a general removal bypass.

### Hierarchy management

- Where approved operation-specific Units authorization permits, support child
  Unit creation, permitted Unit edits, moves, and safe leaf hard deletion;
  provide enough parent/child context to make the affected structure clear.
- Require the creating actor's approved `MANAGE_STRUCTURE` territory and the
  separately required Commander and `MANAGE_AUTHORIZED_USERS` conditions for
  initial Commander assignment. Create the child, root association, Commander,
  and level-0 membership atomically; create no operational grants implicitly.
- Moves remain within one RootUnit, reject cycles/self-descendant destinations,
  move the complete subtree transactionally, preserve direct assignments, and
  recalculate effective inherited authority through current ancestry.
- Follow the approved leaf-only hard-delete and protected-history rules. Reject
  unsafe deletes. Unit archival and subtree deletion are not part of this
  ticket.
- Do not have the UI reconstruct structural authority. Implement the
  operation-specific Units server workflows approved by BCR-20260926-003; do
  not use a generic client-consumable structure boolean as the sole policy.

### RootUnit organization settings

- Provide an organization-settings area available only to the current RootUnit
  owner through the approved narrow Units-owned
  `canManageRootSettings(userId, rootUnitId)`-equivalent.
- Create, list/read, edit, and delete-or-archive that RootUnit's Rank
  definitions; provide usable ordering/sequence where the approved design
  requires it.
- Create, list/read, edit, and delete-or-archive that RootUnit's Medal
  definitions, with enough approved presentation data for future display.
- Child Unit managers do not gain access through ordinary Unit-management
  grants. Do not use `isWebsiteAdmin` as a substitute for RootUnit ownership.
- Rank and Medal exact fields, validation, ordering representation, UI, queries,
  services, and transactions remain GREEN choices after the approved ownership
  and persistence boundary is settled.

  dependent grants ineffective through existing source-revocation rules.

### Hierarchy

- [x] Authorized child Unit creation works with the required initial Commander
      and matching unique level-0 membership in one transaction.
- [x] Structural edits/moves work only for operations inside the approved
      `MANAGE_STRUCTURE` territory and operation-specific server authorization.
- [x] Cross-RootUnit moves, cycles, self/descendant moves, and stale/invalid

- [x] Moves preserve direct grants/memberships and update the moved subtree's
