# Ticket: Refine Player medal sharpness and directional tilt

## Main Success Scenario

1. A visitor hovers a Player medal and sees the original crisp image enlarge
   with its existing neighboring-medal displacement and details.
2. Moving the pointer across the medal tilts its shallow depth in the direction
   of movement, and the medal eases back toward neutral when movement stops.
3. Focus, touch, and reduced-motion behavior remain accessible and unchanged.
