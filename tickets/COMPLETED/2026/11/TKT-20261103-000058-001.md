# Held medal drag

User-authorized adjustment: allow holding and dragging a medal to manually tilt
it, without restoring unpressed pointer-follow behavior.

## Scope

`src/app/globals.css`, `tests/browser/player-award-medal.spec.ts`,
`src/modules/units/MODULE.md`, `src/modules/units/CHANGELOG.md`, and this ticket

## Acceptance criteria

- Primary mouse/pen dragging tilts within horizontal 15 / vertical 12 degree

- Release, cancellation, and capture loss end the drag and return to center.
- A drag does not toggle disclosure or trigger the normal click animation.
- Preserve normal clicks, keyboard activation, idle drift, touch scrolling/taps,
  reduced-motion preferences, and the sharp artwork.
