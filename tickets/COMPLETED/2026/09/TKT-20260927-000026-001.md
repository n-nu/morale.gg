# Ticket: Commander and General Statistics

## Objective

Implement public query-time Commander and General statistics using the shared
Statistics foundation, window policy, and Audits-owned effective-finalized-Audit
source seam established by Ticket 25. Preserve Ticket 24 metric semantics.
Ticket 25 implements the approved Audits source operation, including common
raw observations and command-group descendants; consume it unchanged. Do not
query Audits persistence directly or recreate the source operation in Statistics.
Do not implement Ranker, persistent Unit statistics, attendance, or
UI/leaderboard integration.

## Dependencies

- `TKT-20260927-000024-001` is COMPLETED; architecture and contracts are
  approved.
- `TKT-20260927-000025-001` must be COMPLETED and establish the shared
  Statistics foundation, effective-Audit source seam, and window policy before
  this ticket may move to READY.

## Main Success Scenario

1. A public reader requests Commander or General statistics for an approved
   window.
2. Statistics applies the shared window policy and consumes only the
   Audits-owned effective-finalized-Audit observations.
3. Commander observations reflect battlefield role assignment; General
   observations reflect qualifying command groups and unique flattened atomic
   units.
4. Results preserve raw populations and separate mixed Unit types.

## Requirements

- Use the same effective-Audit seam and window policy established by Ticket 25.
- Commander observations come only from the battlefield Commander role, never
  persistent Unit Commander status. Preserve Audit Unit-type partitioning.
- Commander outputs include battles commanded; total kills, deaths, assists,
  pooled KDR, Tickets, Flag Captures, Flag Losses, and Stars; and average kills,
  deaths, and assists per commanded battle, average KDR derived from the
  approved raw K/D population (never an average of displayed per-battle KDR),
  average Tickets, Flag Captures, Flag Losses, Stars, and Player count.
- General observations require a designated Commander and at least two unique
  descendant atomic units. Nested structures flatten to unique atomic units.
- General cross-type metadata includes qualifying groups commanded, distinct
  Events commanded, total atomic units commanded, and Regular, Rifles, Cavalry,
  and Artillery atomic-unit counts.
- Mixed Regular, Rifles, Cavalry, and Artillery populations remain separate;
  do not recursively average group or Unit averages.
- Compute pooled ratios and averages from raw observations and preserve the
  approved zero-denominator display behavior.
- Keep reads public, query-time, and free of mutable aggregate persistence.
