## Requirements

- Use the same effective-Audit seam and window policy established by Ticket 25.
- Implement the Players-owned membership-history semantic read operation in
  `src/modules/players/**` and the Units-owned hierarchy/subtree semantic read
  operation in `src/modules/units/**`, according to their approved contracts.
  Statistics consumes both operations and does not reproduce membership-period
  or hierarchy semantics.
- Expose Direct Performance, Organizational Performance, and Average Unit
  Performance as distinct perspectives.
- Direct Performance uses raw observations directly represented by the selected
  persistent Unit. Organizational Performance pools raw observations for that
  Unit and all descendants. Average Unit Performance gives equal weight to each
  qualifying Unit's direct raw metrics for additive measures (kills, deaths,
  assists, tickets, flag captures, flag losses, stars, and Player count where
  applicable); never average recursive summaries.
- Average Unit Performance does not average per-Unit KDR values or zero-death
  display forms. KDR remains pooled from raw numerators and denominators for
  Direct and Organizational Performance; Average Unit Performance may omit KDR.
- Preserve historical Audit Unit-type partitions in applicable perspectives.
- Attendance is scoped to Player + persistent Unit + Event. Evaluate
  UnitMembership state at canonical Event occurrence time.
- Create one obligation per eligible Player/Unit/Event when one or more
  mandatory atomic units exist. Set PRESENT immediately if the Player appears
  in any finalized mandatory Audit for that Unit/Event within the Audits-owned
  effective result set.
- Set PENDING when an obligation exists, the Player is not PRESENT, and at
  least one mandatory atomic unit lacks an effective finalized Audit.
- Set ABSENT only when all mandatory atomic units have authoritative finalized
  Audit outcomes and the Player appeared in none. Exclude PENDING obligations
  from the resolved attendance percentage.
- Mercenary Audit appearances do not create roster obligations or satisfy
  another Unit's obligation. Separate active memberships create separate
  obligations.
- Keep reads public, query-time, and free of mutable aggregate persistence.

## Average Unit Performance Ratio Semantics

Equal-Unit averages apply to additive raw measures: kills, deaths, assists,
tickets, flag captures, flag losses, stars, and Player count where applicable.
Do not calculate Average Unit Performance KDR by averaging per-Unit KDR values,
and do not average zero-death display forms such as `12 K`. KDR remains a
pooled raw numerator/denominator statistic for Direct and Organizational
Performance. Average Unit Performance may omit KDR rather than expose a
misleading or duplicate ratio. Ratios are never recursively or arithmetically
averaged.
